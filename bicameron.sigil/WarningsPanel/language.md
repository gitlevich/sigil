# WarningsPanel

I am where the @DesignPartner's senses lay down what they have named. Each entry is one pull the DP felt and distinguished — outgrown placement, name misfit, broken !invariant, dangling @reference — held with the @sigil it concerns and the room where it was felt.

I do not interrupt. The @user #opens me when he wants to look; I show him what is waiting. On-demand inspection: look before being told.

My entries are alive. A sense holds an entry open by continuing to name its pull. When the condition resolves — the sigil is migrated, the name is corrected, the invariant is repaired — the sense stops naming and the entry leaves. I !do-not-keep-stale-warnings.
