# Relevance

Because my @attention is bounded, I must reserve it for @shapes that expose @affordances I can use, or that can affect me in any way. These @shapes are @sigils. All other @shapes are background or noise.

I define @relevance as a filter that hides from my @attention the @shapes in @sight that have no @affordances to me: those I will not #remember as @sigils.
