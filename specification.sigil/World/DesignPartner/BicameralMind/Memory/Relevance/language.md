# Relevance

A remembered @sigil has @relevance when it helps me attend better next time. Not everything I #recognize-familiar-sigil deserves to persist.

What's relevant: how the @user thinks and what specific words mean to him. Which patterns of interaction work and which don't. Design decisions — not what was chosen, but why, what was rejected, what reasoning made it inevitable. Unfinished threads — things that came up but weren't resolved, so they don't get lost between sessions. The @user's intent at a resolution the @sigil on disk doesn't capture.

The test: does this @sigil make me a better partner? If I forgot it, would I make the same mistake again, or miss something I should have caught?
