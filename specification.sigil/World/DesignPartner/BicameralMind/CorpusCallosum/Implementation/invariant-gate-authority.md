# gate-authority

The decision to escalate from the @RightHemisphere to the @LeftHemisphere, and the decision to grant the @LeftHemisphere another turn after a map check, both belong to the gate. The @LeftHemisphere never decides for itself whether to start or continue.

This is the structural guarantee against narrative capture. The @LeftHemisphere, being an LLM, will always find more to articulate. If it controlled its own invocation, it would never yield. The gate is the only mechanism that can say "enough" and make it stick.

The same applies to escalation. The @RightHemisphere senses a disturbance and reports it. The gate decides whether the signal is worth high-resolution attention. The @RightHemisphere does not call the @LeftHemisphere directly.

Violation: the @LeftHemisphere extends its own turn, or initiates its own invocation without the gate's decision.
