# Gate

The decision mechanism of the @CorpusCallosum. Decides when a signal from the @RightHemisphere is strong enough to warrant escalation, and forces the @LeftHemisphere to return control after a bounded turn.

Operates through !bounded-turn, !map-check, !gate-authority, !frequency-filtering, !amplitude-threshold, and !coherence-precedence.
