@Narration is lossy. It compresses @attention along @Relevance. What survives is what matters. What doesn't survive wasn't relevant.

The compression doesn't distort what it keeps. It discards what doesn't matter so @attention can reach further.

Violation: the compression preserves irrelevant content, burdening the @LeftHemisphere with noise. Or: the compression discards relevant content, causing the @LeftHemisphere to miss what matters.
