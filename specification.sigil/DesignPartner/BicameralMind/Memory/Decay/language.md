# Decay

What happens to a remembered @sigil's own @Resolution when the @Path to it stops being walked. The sphere loses definition in @CoOccurrenceGeometry; #recognize-familiar-sigil reaches for it and finds a fuzzier shape, until eventually the shape is too weak to be recognized at all.

@Resolution is distinct from @Path's precision. Precision is how finely the approach was made; @Resolution is how sharply the destination is still held. Decay governs the second axis.

!passive-decay is the mechanism: graph connectivity thins as the living @Memory grows past an unreinforced @sigil. #forget is the end of that thinning. @Consolidation and @Recognition are what counteract it.
