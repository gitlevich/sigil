# relevance-gating

Active @invariants are scoped to the currently open @sigil. The @user and @DesignPartner share this scope. A burst of activity is relevant when it involves @sigils entangled with these @invariants — co-occurrence in sentences, direct only.

Bursts outside the active scope stay quiet even if geometrically significant, unless they involve @sigils that are entangled with the active @invariants.

Violation: a burst involving unentangled @sigils interrupts the current work. Or: a burst involving @sigils directly entangled with active @invariants goes unnoticed.
