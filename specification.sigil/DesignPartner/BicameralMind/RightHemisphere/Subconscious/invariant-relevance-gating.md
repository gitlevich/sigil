# relevance-gating

Active @invariants are scoped to the currently open @sigil. The @user and @DesignPartner share this scope.

A burst of activity is relevant when it involves @sigils entangled with these @invariants — co-occurrence in sentences, direct only. Three sources of entanglement carry different weight:

Children — always relevant. This is the work itself.

Neighbors — relevant when the current @sigil references their @affordances. A neighbor's affordance changing is a dependency risk.

Parent — always relevant. Its @affordances are laws of nature for the current @sigil. A parent change affecting active @invariants is an earthquake.

Violation: a burst involving unentangled @sigils interrupts the current work. Or: a parent affordance change that affects active @invariants goes unnoticed.
