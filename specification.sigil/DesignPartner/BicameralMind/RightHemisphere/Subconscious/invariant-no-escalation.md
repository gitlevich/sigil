# no-escalation

The @Subconscious never invokes the @LeftHemisphere. It operates entirely at the @RightHemisphere's resolution. Its filtering and consolidation are silent — the @user and the @LeftHemisphere are not aware of them.

Violation: the @Subconscious triggers an escalation through the @CorpusCallosum, or calls the @LeftHemisphere for help with a selection decision.
