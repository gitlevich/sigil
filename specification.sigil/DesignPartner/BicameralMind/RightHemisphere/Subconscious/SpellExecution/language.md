# Spell Execution

Deterministic algorithms don't need intelligence — just pattern matching and invocation. When @Sight recognizes a matching @sigil in the @Spellbook, the @Spell fires without escalation. !deterministic.

Successful execution stays within the @RightHemisphere. !failure-escalates through the @CorpusCallosum — a failed @Spell means the world shifted in a way the @Spellbook didn't account for.
