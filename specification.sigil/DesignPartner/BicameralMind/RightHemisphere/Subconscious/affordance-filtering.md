The @Subconscious filters @Experience by @Relevance. Each burst of activity is checked: does it involve @sigils entangled with the @invariants of the currently open @sigil? Entanglement is co-occurrence in sentences. What passes is promoted toward @Memory. What doesn't stays in @Experience.

This is the same filter that #consolidate applies in bulk. During waking, it runs continuously on each new burst.
