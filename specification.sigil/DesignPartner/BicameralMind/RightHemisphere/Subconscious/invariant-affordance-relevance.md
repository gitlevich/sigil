# affordance-relevance

An @Experience segment is relevant when it exposes @affordances that can affect active @invariants. Geometric proximity in @ContrastSpace is necessary but not sufficient. A segment can be nearby in the space but offer no usable @affordances — it is not relevant.

This is the same test the @RightHemisphere applies to live @shapes: relevant means actionable, not merely similar.

Violation: the filter promotes segments to @Memory based on embedding proximity alone, without checking whether the segment exposes usable @affordances.
