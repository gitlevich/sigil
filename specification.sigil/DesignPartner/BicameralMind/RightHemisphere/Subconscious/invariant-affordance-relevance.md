# affordance-relevance

An @Experience segment is relevant when it involves @sigils whose @affordances can affect the active @invariants of the currently open @sigil. Co-occurrence in sentences establishes entanglement. Proximity alone is not sufficient — a @sigil can be nearby in @ContrastSpace but offer no @affordances that touch the active @invariants.

Relevant means actionable, not merely similar.

Violation: the filter promotes segments to @Memory based on proximity alone, without checking whether the segment's @sigils have @affordances entangled with the active @invariants.
