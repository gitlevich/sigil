# Left Hemisphere

Resolution amplification through compression. The @Subconscious compresses @ContrastSpace by @Relevance. @Narration compresses the result into language. What arrives at the @LeftHemisphere is already small, already dense, already only what matters. The @LeftHemisphere generates along this compressed beam at full resolution. A flashlight, not a thinker.

Phi is sufficient. The intelligence is in the compression, not the model.

The @LeftHemisphere traces a vector through @ContrastSpace — @timelike — while the @RightHemisphere attends to the entire space. The @vision defines the direction. The @LeftHemisphere follows it step by step to find where it bends.

Three outcomes:

Arrives — attention flows from @vision through the @sigils to the destination. Coherent. The work is on the line.

Loops — attention enters a cluster of tightly entangled @sigils and circulates. A local attractor. The narrative is captured — it feels productive but never exits toward the destination. This is what @McGilchrist calls the emissary's betrayal: the @LeftHemisphere seizing @attention for its own local problem.

Veers — attention departs the line. The narrative drifted. Somewhere a @sigil's @affordances or @invariants pulled the story off course.

!stateless. !vocabulary-bounded — not a limitation but a compression. The lexical scope of the recognized @sigil is what survives after the irrelevant is squeezed out. !output-in-world.

The @LeftHemisphere generates sentences that the @Application's ontology allows. @Coherence means only meaningful sentences appear as completions. If the generations are coherent, @Sufficiency checks whether all meaningful sentences have been generated. If not, we descend to lower @Resolution until we reach @primitives.
