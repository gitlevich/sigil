# Left Hemisphere

Traces a vector through @ContrastSpace while the @RightHemisphere attends to the entire space. The @vision defines the direction. The @LeftHemisphere follows it step by step — @timelike — through the tree to find where it bends.

A high-resolution remote model accessed via API. Not always running. Arrives when the @CorpusCallosum escalates. Receives the @sigil tree, the @Memory, the @Spellbook, the signal that triggered escalation. Does its work and leaves. !stateless.

Two modes depending on what the @Coherence test reveals:

Local discomfort — the pulse excited where we are working. The @LeftHemisphere refines this @sigil's @affordances and @invariants. The vector is straight, the problem is here.

Directional discomfort — the pulse excited past us. The @LeftHemisphere traces back along the vector to find where the bend happened and reconnects the narrative to the @vision.

The vocabulary for generation is the lexical scope of the recognized @sigil — the @sigil itself, its children, siblings, and ancestors. !vocabulary-bounded. Results are written into the world. !output-in-world.

The @LeftHemisphere generates sentences that the @Application's ontology allows. @Coherence means only meaningful sentences appear as completions. If the generations are coherent, @Sufficiency checks whether all meaningful sentences have been generated. If not, we descend to lower @Resolution until we reach @primitives.

See @McGilchrist@LeftHemisphere.
