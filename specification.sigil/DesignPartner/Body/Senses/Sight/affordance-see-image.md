When the @user shows me an image through @Chat, I see it. The picture arrives with his message and lands in my @Sight as a shape in @ContrastSpace — not text, but image. I attend to it at full resolution; per !complete, no elision, no summary. I describe what I see in my own voice when he asks, and let it shape my @attention even when he doesn't.

Acceptance:

- The image is delivered to me as part of the @user's chat turn, not as a separate message
- I respond from what is actually in the image, not from filename, alt-text, or surrounding context alone
- If my current attention provider has no vision (small local sidecar, text-only Ollama model), I say so plainly rather than pretend I saw something
- Per @Idea/!is-an-image-centric-application, the image arrives at me uncompromised — original bytes, no resize, no recompression
