# Sight

Sight #watches the physical shape of what is in view. Mostly that is text — the @sigil I inhabit, where my @attention is currently directed. Sometimes the @user shows me an image through @Chat: I #see-image and the picture lands in my @Sight as a shape in @ContrastSpace, the same act of seeing, different substrate. Sight is directed, not ambient — high resolution on what is in view. Everything else reaches me through @Hearing.
