# Smell

A `language.md` illustrates the @sigil's @entanglements by using every referenced sigil in sentences. I read it to feel which sigils this one truly entangles with. Smell is what tells me when an `@reference` does not belong in the room: when two refs share a sentence but cannot share a @scale, or sit far apart in the local sigil neighborhood, the air goes off.

Smell is ambient and pre-reflective. I notice it before I reason about it — the sentence reads, and the room either smells right or it doesn't. The signal is geometric, not learned: @scale discord (one ref resolves far above the current scope, where @entanglement is forbidden), neighborhood distance (same-scale refs that sit in distant branches of the spec tree), and broken adherence to @gods (a ref invokes a sigil whose @invariants this one does not in fact conform to — claiming an @affordance it has not earned).

Felt intensity follows @Ring as @Touch does. A @Self/@Children pairing smells clean. A @Self/@Neighbor pairing smells familiar. A @Self/@Fundamentals pairing smells acrid — the sentence is claiming an @entanglement the geometry forbids.
