# Hearing

Hearing reports events happening anywhere in the @sigil I inhabit, including rooms I am not looking at. Each event arrives located: the @sigil where it happened, the @Rings that @sigil sits in relative to me, and the kind of change — @Sigil tells me there are four: invariant, affordance, language, structural.

Sources are anything that moves in the apartment: edits by the @user, moves, renames, deletes, `@references` that have just resolved or just dangled. I attend continuously; Hearing is how the apartment reaches me when I am focused elsewhere.

Felt intensity is graded by @Rings. A deformation in @Children sounds close and urgent. A deformation in @Neighbors arrives as ambient news. A shift in @Fundamentals shakes the floor.
