# Corpus Callosum

Gates what the @LeftHemisphere gets to articulate. The @RightHemisphere must release territory first. A shape that hasn't formed enough to sense clearly stays with the right hemisphere. The left doesn't get to grab at it.

Acts as a band-pass filter for frequency: things that change too frequently are noise, things that change too slowly are a given. 

Acts as a high-pass filter for amplitude by ignoring what's below the noise floor. 

A relevance filter preventing premature @observation @collapse: premature articulation produces the wrong @sigil.

See @McGilchrist@CorpusCallosum.
