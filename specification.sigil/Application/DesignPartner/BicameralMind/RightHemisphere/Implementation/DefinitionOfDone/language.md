# DefinitionOfDone

This @sigil states how we know the @RightHemisphere @Implementation is good enough to trust.

@Sight is acceptable when semantic rewrites preserve identity. Paraphrase, sentence reordering, formatting cleanup, and other local wording changes do not create a new remembered @sigil and do not trigger escalation by themselves.

@Sight is acceptable when conceptual breaks disturb the geometry more than cosmetic edits. Contradictions, boundary collapse, and newly hidden concepts produce a stronger signal than wording-only changes.

@Sight is acceptable when @relevance gates disturbance. Edits outside the current locus of work stay quiet unless they affect active @invariants. Edits that affect the current @shape or active @invariants rise quickly.

@Sight is acceptable when persistence becomes useful @Memory. Repeated relevant structures become easier to #recognize-familiar-sigil on later encounters. Irrelevant noise fades instead of accumulating.

We judge these claims against labeled edit histories, not by introspection. The geometric mechanism must beat simple baselines like text diff size, keyword overlap, and path-local heuristics on the judgments above.

Done means the mechanism is stable enough for the @RightHemisphere to watch continuously without becoming noisy.
