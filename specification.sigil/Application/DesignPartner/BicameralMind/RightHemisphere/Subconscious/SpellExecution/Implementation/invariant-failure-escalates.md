# failure-escalates

A failed spell is a disturbance. The conditions matched but execution could not complete. Something in the world shifted in a way the @Spellbook didn't account for. The @Subconscious cannot resolve this — it escalates through the @CorpusCallosum to the @LeftHemisphere.

Successful execution stays silent within the @RightHemisphere. Only failure escalates.

Violation: a spell fails silently and no escalation occurs. The shifted world goes unnoticed.
