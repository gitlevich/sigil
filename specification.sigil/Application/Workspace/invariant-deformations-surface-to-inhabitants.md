When the @sigil tree deforms — a @reference dangles or resolves, a name shadows, an #invariant breaks, a shape moves — the Workspace reports the deformation located in the room where it happened, felt by every inhabitant of that room.

The @DesignPartner receives structured events via @Body @Senses (@Touch in-gesture, @Hearing ambient). The @User receives the same deformations as visible giving, audible cues, and subtle tilts in the UI. Same source, different readouts. Neither inhabitant lives in an apartment that keeps its own deformations secret.
