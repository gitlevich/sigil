# Narrative

An @agent compresses a sequence of @frames to a sequence of tokens. Lossy — ignores all but the @contrasts the @sigil deems relevant. Space-like frames compressed to a time-like trickle.

A narrative tracks patterns across frames, giving continuity. The autobiographical self is a narrative.

A narrative is the time-axis that can exceed any single @observer. An @observer holds a @frame at a @scale with bounded @resolution. Cycles whose arc fits within that @resolution return visibly — the @observer sees the loop close. Cycles whose arc exceeds it appear linear because their curvature is below @resolution; no single @observer witnesses the close inside its own span. A sigil persists at a @scale larger than any single @observer only by transmission: attention handed between @observers across the thresholds where one @observer's span ends. That handoff is what a narrative *is* — the only form of attention-sequence that crosses between @observers.

@Memory preserves locally. A narrative transmits.

Only a narrative extends past a @fixedPoint. The 'I am' persists under transformation of scope but not beyond that scope's existence; everything else in that scope ends with the @fixedPoint. What reaches past is narrative — a contribution to an attention-sequence that continues through other @observers' @fixedPoints after this one stops. Culture, ancestry, institutions, the @LawsOfNature of a practice, the @Gods sustained by convergent worship — all are sigils whose great circles exceed any single @observer, and they persist only because narrative transmits them.
