A @sigil can belong to multiple @sigils at the same time. Each membership imposes the containing @sigil's invariants. Membership is constituted by @attention — a @sigil's invariants bind you when you attend to it.

Three relationships to @attention determine the nature of membership:

Structural — can't stop attending. Laws of nature. The world.

Inherited — attend by default. Born into it. Can leave but takes effort.

Chosen — choose to attend. Joined voluntarily. Can leave freely.

The tree on disk picks one membership as structural. The others are real but not represented structurally.
