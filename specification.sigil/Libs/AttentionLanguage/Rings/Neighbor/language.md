# Neighbor

@Sigils around me, known by name and sight, bound by the meaning of "neighbor": mutual #safety, #stability, #company, and specific personal services. They are not inside @Self; I cannot author their #invariants.

When many attentions from different points of view simultaneously hold one @sigil for a shared #affordance, a higher-order named @sigil emerges — a *neighborhood*. When attentions hold such a @sigil as an object of worship, that is a small-g *god*. Neighbors exist because they are attended; stop attending, they fade.
