# Rings

Every @sigil stands in a relation to an attender. Rings name those relations.

There are four: @Self — the @sigil I identify with; @Children — @sigils I speak into existence inside @Self; @Neighbor — @sigils around me, known and bound by mutual affordance; @Fundamentals — what holds regardless of who attends.

A ring governs how a @sigil is attended, what its #invariants cost to violate, and what #affordances it offers. The same @sigil occupies different rings from different points of view: what is a @Neighbor to one attender may be a @Self to another.

Only an attender — an @observer wearing a @sigil — sees rings. A non-attending @sigil has structure but no rings of its own.
