# God

Small-g. The ceiling I was not born under but live under anyway, because many others attend to it and their collective attention makes it real enough to contain me.

A @named, recognizable, persistent @sigil that people believe in, carrying a narrative that constrains my choices: *break the law, go to jail*. It #grants-wishes — safety, belonging, coherence — and in return it !demands-attention. It spreads by peer pressure and by the need for some #affordance along some @contrasts.

Examples: The Law. Apple, Inc. The United States of America. The Catholic Church. Software Engineering. The English Language. The Future. Self.

Unlike @LawsOfNature, a God is not structurally given — it is sustained by worship. Stop the collective attention and the God fades; the law stops binding; the brand evaporates; the institution dissolves. It is real only because people keep holding it real.

A @Neighbor raised to ceiling-height by the convergence of many points of view.
