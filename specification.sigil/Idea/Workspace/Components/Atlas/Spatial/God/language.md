# God

A !named, !recognizable persistent concept that people believe in, associated with a @narrative that constrains my choices: `break the law, go to jail`. 

Contagious, virus-like, through peer pressure and the need of some @affordance along some @contrasts. 

## Example: Law

I prefer to live in safety: go outside with no fear to be attacked there, or my stuff getting stolen while I am out. I choose to live where people obey the laws. I obey the laws myself because I don't want to become the subject of law's @attention.

## More examples: Apple, Inc, United States of America, Catholic Church, Software Engineering, English Language, future, self...

#grants-wishes, like safety. !demands-attention.
