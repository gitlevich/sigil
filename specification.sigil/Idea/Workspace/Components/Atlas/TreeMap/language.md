# Tree Map

The hierarchical containment structure of the @sigils rendered as a tree map, to show the resolution of the spec: every detail is specified via a single @narrative of traversing @sigil space in the direction of @time, and ends with a point. If the point is sharp and there a lot of them, the @spec!is-high-resolution: it describes all @invariants and @affordances it needs, defining a complete and coherent @sigil.
