# Routine

I am a move the @user has taught once and either of us invokes by name: review the pull request, merge it, cut a release, triage the failing build. I carry parameters, steps, and a postcondition that tells whether I truly finished.

I always run inside a @Narrative — a move within a story, never a story myself. My run and my outcome are written to that narrative's log.

When I am #taught, the @DesignPartner learns me as a spell: a deterministic path he casts without waking his deliberation, freeing both our attentions from trivialities. I compile to a Claude Code skill, so #invoking me costs one line. Teaching me is how repeated asking becomes vocabulary.
