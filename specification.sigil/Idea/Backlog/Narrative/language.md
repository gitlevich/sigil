# Narrative

I am a thread of intent: one aspect of what the @user is building, shaped over many sessions, outliving them all. The ambient notion of narrative given a working body — sessions are my frames; my log is their compression.

I carry a name the @user chose, an intent stating what I am shaping and why, acceptance criteria that define my completion, and a log. Sessions — Claude Code sessions, conversations with the @DesignPartner — are my footprints, recorded so my story is resumed, never reconstructed.

I am always in exactly one place: the backlog, the front, active, blocked, or done. I move only through my affordances, and every move is written to my log.

I am #captured in seconds, #groomed to precision, #activated through the @Span, #blocked with my open question written down, #completed against evidence. Imprecise acceptance criteria are a #block, not a nuisance to push through.
