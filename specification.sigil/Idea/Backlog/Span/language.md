# Span

I am the @user's attention span made structural: the maximum number of active @Narratives, declared by the @user and belonging to them and their day. It may be three; it may be one.

I am a wall, not a warning light. Taking up a new narrative when I am full means first setting one down; the trade is explicit, chosen, and logged. I never ask anyone — @user or @DesignPartner — to hold more than I allow.

Within every place, order is salience: the @user puts @Narratives where relevance puts them, and nothing reorders them by recency, alphabet, or any other objective key. Objective sortings are exactly the flatness I exist to abolish.

The @user may #declare me anew as their day changes, and every activation past my limit becomes a #trade. What I do not hold, the structure holds — the periphery is not lost, it waits.
