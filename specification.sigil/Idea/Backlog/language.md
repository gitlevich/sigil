# Backlog

Work in time. The @Workspace holds the space-like view of the @Idea — where I stand, and move laterally between siblings or vertically between abstractions, always at the same bounded complexity. The Backlog holds the time-like view: @Narratives of work flowing through a bounded @Span of attention toward done.

A @Narrative here is the ambient notion given a body: a thread of work that outlives every session held in its name. It survives each session boundary by transmission — its intent, acceptance criteria, and log brief whoever arrives next, the @user or the @DesignPartner. Nothing is re-explained; it is read.

I #survey the whole field in a glance: active narratives nearest and largest, the front next, blocked ones carrying their open questions, the backlog folded but reachable, done receding. Moving here costs what moving in the sigil tree costs — one bounded step: laterally between @Narratives, or forward through one.

A @Routine is a move within a narrative, never a narrative itself.

The board is !visually-differentiated: recognition happens before reading.
