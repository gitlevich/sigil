### Assistant (20:56 UTC)

This is the DKIM setup step. In Squarespace DNS:

1. Go to **Squarespace** → **Domains** → **sigilengineering.com** → **DNS Settings**
2. Add a **TXT record**:
   - **Host**: `google._domainkey`
   - **Data**: paste the full `v=DKIM1;k=rsa;p=...` value from that page
   - **TTL**: lowest available
3. Go back to Google Workspace and click **Start authentication** (it may take up to 48h to propagate, but often works within minutes)

Note: Squarespace may not support 2048-bit DKIM keys due to TXT record length limits. If the record fails to save, go back to Google and select **1024-bit** key length, then generate a new shorter key and use that instead.
