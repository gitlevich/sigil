Graphify is a descriptive indexing layer. It extracts code, documents, diagrams, and rationale into nodes and provenance-tagged edges, then supports neighborhood, path, and explanation queries. Its communities are discovered algorithmically from topology using Leiden/Louvain clustering. [Graphify’s README](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify/blob/v8/README.md#what-it-does), [graph schema](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify/blob/v8/ARCHITECTURE.md#extraction-output-schema), and [clustering implementation](https://github.com/Graphify-Labs/graphify/blob/v8/graphify/cluster.py#L122-L145) confirm this.

A sigil imposes a constitutive grammar on that substrate. It must be recursive and inhabitable; its boundary exposes affordances and protects invariants; it introduces lexical scope and deterministic name resolution; and its structure must be projectable into code without changing shape or vocabulary. Those are not annotations on an arbitrary graph. They determine which graphs are legal sigils: [Sigil](/Users/vlad/sigil/specification.sigil/Libs/AttentionLanguage/Sigil/language.md:7), [Boundary](/Users/vlad/sigil/specification.sigil/Libs/AttentionLanguage/Sigil/Boundary/language.md:7), [Scope](/Users/vlad/sigil/specification.sigil/Libs/AttentionLanguage/Sigil/Scope/language.md:7), [Name Resolution](/Users/vlad/sigil/specification.sigil/Libs/AttentionLanguage/Sigil/NameResolution/language.md:7), [projectability](/Users/vlad/sigil/specification.sigil/Idea/language.md:7), and [shape/name correspondence](/Users/vlad/sigil/architectural_invariants.md:3).
